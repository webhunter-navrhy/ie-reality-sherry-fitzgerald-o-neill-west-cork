Description

Situated on the outskirts of Skibbereen town in West Cork, this three bed detached property with outbuildings, set on 0.5 acre approximately offers an excellent opportunity for those seeking a renovation project in a convenient and well-established location.

The property extends to 100 sq. m. (1,074 sq. ft.) approx., and provides well-proportioned accommodation over two floors. The ground floor comprises an entrance hallway, sitting room, living room, kitchen and guest WC, while upstairs there are three bedrooms and a family bathroom. There is also a separate utility room.

To the rear, there are numerous outbuildings including a garage and shed.

The property now requires renovation and refurbishment throughout, offering considerable scope for the successful purchaser to modernise and upgrade the accommodation to their own requirements. Given its vacant condition and the nature of the works required, the property may qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant and potentially the Derelict Property Refurbishment Grant, subject to the relevant eligibility criteria and approval by the local authority.

The location is a particular feature of the property, being positioned on the outskirts of Skibbereen, while remaining within easy reach of the town's range of shops, cafés, restaurants, schools, services and amenities. The surrounding area also provides convenient access to West Cork's coastline and many of the region's popular villages and attractions.

This property offers an excellent opportunity to acquire a substantial site with a three-bedroom house and the potential to create a comfortable home in a highly desirable West Cork location.
